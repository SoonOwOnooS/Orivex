# Orivex

[中文](README.md) · [Visit the website](https://orivex.real-videogame.workers.dev/)

## Purpose

Orivex aims to help players find and support developers who love making games. We want the people, stories, and ideas behind games to be easier to discover.

The idea comes from a concern about games being copied quickly. By keeping public records and following a game's creative journey, we hope to help players learn how it began and find work they want to support.

## Planned content

These are the areas we plan to develop:

1. **Creation stories:** Why a developer made the game, where the ideas came from, and how the design changed.
2. **Evidence timelines:** Social posts, dev logs, and first showcases, with source links and room for additions and corrections. We plan to let users signed in with GitHub check these records and help show the earliest known public dates.
3. **Fan culture:** Fan sections for games or series, bringing together fan works with creator credit and original links. Official content and fan works would be clearly marked.

We hope players will find Orivex through community sharing, learn about a game's story, design ideas, and fan culture, and decide which games and creators they want to support.
